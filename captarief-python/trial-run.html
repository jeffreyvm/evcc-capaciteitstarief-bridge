Controls

The control law is line-for-line the same as core.py: target = max(monthly peak, 2500) − margin, envelope = min(target, remaining budget ÷ remaining time), clamped to the target. The budgeter recalculates every 30 s and publishes on ≥50 W change or after a 30 s heartbeat. evcc controls within that envelope every second, with 6 A as the floor and 300 W/s as the ramp rate — the latter is an assumption, not a measured value.
